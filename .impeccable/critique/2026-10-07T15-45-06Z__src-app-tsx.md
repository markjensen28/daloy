## Overall impression

There’s a memorable water-planning workspace here, but its default state makes the scene easier to notice than the planning result. The biggest opportunity is to put the correct scope and the most important figures directly beside the model, then use color and labels to clarify what is illustrative and what each number represents.

## What’s working

- The miniature infrastructure scene gives the product a distinct anchor and makes water planning feel spatial.
- Controls are grouped by Sources, Demand, and Policies, with labeled values and immediate updates.
- Impact details include household burden, assistance, unmet demand, and an explicit note that affected-household counts are estimates.

Keep “Illustrative inputs · one-day balance · no live utility data” visible near the results. **Suggested command:** `$impeccable clarify`.
